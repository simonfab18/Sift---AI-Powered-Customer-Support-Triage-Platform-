# Legal Review Checklist

This document is a product and engineering checklist. It is not legal advice and does not mark the product as legally approved. A qualified legal reviewer or an authorized business owner must approve public legal terms before a commercial launch.

## Current Status

- Status: pending external legal review.
- Product mode: free pilot only.
- Paid billing: intentionally deferred.
- Direct sending: not enabled; approved replies create Gmail drafts only.
- Self-serve data export: implemented for owners/admins from Settings -> Readiness.
- Deletion request intake: implemented for owners from Settings -> Readiness; hard deletion is not self-serve yet.

- Marketing and pricing copy that mentions free pilot access or future plans.

## Topics To Confirm Before Public Launch

1. Controller/processor roles for Gmail support data.
2. Exact data categories processed from Gmail, tickets, attachments, AI prompts, and audit logs.
3. User consent and Gmail OAuth scope language.
4. Data export response window and delivery channel.
5. Organization deletion request workflow, retention window, and exceptions.
6. Backup retention and when deleted data ages out of backups.
7. Audit-log retention for security and abuse prevention.
8. Incident notification process and support contact expectations.
9. AI disclosure language for generated triage and drafts.
10. Free pilot terms, warranty disclaimers, and no-SLA language.
11. Future paid-plan terms before any billing or subscription feature ships.
